The technique has four moves:

1. Pick one concept and write its name at the top of a page.
2. Explain it as if to someone new to the field, in plain words.
3. Mark every place you hesitated or used jargon to hide a gap.
4. Return to the source for those gaps, then simplify again.

The part that matters is step three. Fluency can feel like understanding; hesitation is the honest signal.
